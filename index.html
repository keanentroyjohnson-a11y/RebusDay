How to play

A rebus is a word picture. Doodles, and where words sit, how big they are, which way they face and what color they are, all spell out a word or phrase.

  • Three new puzzles every day: Starter, Tricky and Brain-bender. Want a go first? Tap Warm up for extra puzzles that don't count.
  • Say the doodles out loud. A bee might mean "be", an eye might mean "I".
  • No letter boxes: just type the whole phrase. You get 3 guesses, and any word you get right in the right place locks in.
  • Stuck? Take a clue and scratch it off, then reveal first letters.
  • You start the day on 1,000 points. A wrong guess costs 50, a clue 50, first letters another 100, and a revealed or missed puzzle 250. The clock starts when you press Play and costs 1 point a second (up to 250 per puzzle).
  • Tap the drawing to watch it sketch again.
